Do you wanna hold hands in the mocha?

Oct. 28, 2025

"Do you want to hold hands in the mocha?"

She asks me, almost expectantly.

"Hold hands in the mocha?"

I reply, horrified.

"Yeah, it's just, there's a lot of it left, but it's expired now. There's enough that we can hold hands together!" She pulls the pump off the top of the container, revealing, as promised, a lot of thick, expired chocolate sauce.

She explains the idea of holding hands in the mocha so casually. As though the part that wasn't computing to me was the "mocha" part.

"No, no. I get that," I respond, "It's just. Isn't that a little intimate?"

I say this like an accusation. 'How disgusting! You want to be intimate?!?! With me?!?!'. She notices my discomfort.

"What would we do while we held hands in the mocha?" I follow up quickly, "Do I have to look at you? Are you going to look at me?"

I can't tell if she’s offended or amused.

"It is intimate," She says casually. "Jess told me she was pregnant while we held hands in the mocha."

'No! This is terrible. She’s held hands with other women in the mocha?!?' I think to myself.

"That's disgusting. Absolutely not." I say.

"Fine. I'll save it until Jess gets here then, she's in at 6pm."

My heart shatters.

She walks away nonchalantly.

Where to next?!
