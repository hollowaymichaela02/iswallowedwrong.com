I sleep with eyeliner on.

May 28, 2025

I'm staring into the bathroom that my brother’s supposed to be in, peeking around the doorframe to find him.

Not sure why I didn't just walk in, the door was open. Last I checked he was in there: Is he hiding?

He's supposed to be waiting for me to help do his hair.

"Why did you just de-spawn?" I call out to him.

"Sorry about that," he says, stepping out of liminal space with a smirk.

"What the hell, man?" I respond, still brushing my hair. I walk into the bathroom and begin gesturing towards myself. "Come here." I say with a silly accent.

It's 6:47am on Wednesday.

Why are we both awake? How does he know I'm awake?

I mean, I'm usually awake but I didn't notice that he noticed.

Tyler is 12. At least, I think. He recently discovered that he can try new things with his hair, doesn't have to just flatten it to one side (see: Donald Trump comb over).

I'm really proud that he feels safe enough to come to me for help. Even though he doesn't say it, doesn't even ask, really. He'll just walk up to me at 6:47am with a bottle of Kristin Ess Sea Salt Air Dry Mousse clenched in one fist and stare blankly.

"Your hair looks nice," I say.

I just woke up five minutes ago. I'm sitting outside with my dog in 10 degree weather. It's a little cold and my tank top has skeleton hands that look like they're holding my boobs. A part of me feels self conscious about that, the other hasn't even woken up yet. But first: "What did you do differently?"

"What do you mean?" He says, accusatory.

"It looks fluffier than usual." I shrug, pointing to the fluffy hair.

"I just woke up." Face still blank.

"Oh." I say, moving to stand, "Hold on a second."

Why am I writing about this, you ask?

Well. Because… it's an oddly vulnerable moment disguised as something we’re comfortable with.

Why does my brother hide in liminal space when I leave the room for 15 seconds?

Why do I feel insecure in my pajamas at 6:47am and start to think that I'm ugly?

I sleep with eyeliner on.

That doesn't mean I don't wash my face, it means I have really fucking awesome eyeliner that doesn't come off with measly old face wash.

I sleep with eyeliner on so when I wake up in the morning I won't be upset by the shape of my own eyeballs.

But most importantly, I sleep with eyeliner on so when my brother approaches me five minutes after I wake up, he doesn't have to face a cruel, mean ogre.

It's thoughtful, really.

I hope he knows how much I love him.
