My first Oktoberfest experience.

October 23rd, 2026
Street performers Seb and Fritz at Oktoberfest in Kitchener, ON. Taken October 6th, 2026. Video by Michaela Holloway.

Seb and Frtiz are German street performers. Their stage presence is both painfully awkward and charmingly charismatic. The performance was completely absurd. I can confidently say this was the first (and likely last) time I have watched a homoerotic whip performance with another man beatboxing to boost atmosphere. It was awesome. You just had to be there.

"Not sure what the hell I'm looking at here."

"Guy beatboxing while man standing on his head whips to the beat."
