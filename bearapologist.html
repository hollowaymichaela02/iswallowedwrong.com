I'm a Bear Apologist [Obsession].

May 23, 2026

I find the takes about Bear in Obsession to be really fucking brain dead.

He was an insecure man who wished upon a toy for his crush to love him and that got his crush possessed by a demon. Right.

I don't think Bear was a villain! I'll say it! He was sad and desperate. Sometimes, sad, desperate people do sad, desperate things.

I once tried to do a black magic ritual to get my crush to like me back.

Guess what happened?

Nothing.

'Cause that's what usually happens when you make wishes.

I mean, c'mon, he never could have reasonably predicted something so extreme would happen.

Not to mention, he couldn't realistically fix anything unless he killed himself or her.

Both of which are not viable solutions.

Yeah, something is seriously wrong with her and he clearly notices that. It's kinda fucked up that he just accepts it. But what the fuck is he supposed to do? This shit is totally unprecedented.

"He shouldn't have made such a selfish wish in the first place."

Oh! That's an awesome suggestion.

Unfortunately that would only be proof of his evil mind HAD HE KNOWN THE WISH WOULD COME TRUE.

I mean, GUYS. Just think about it.

Do I think Bear liked the idea of her more than he ever actually liked her? 100%

Do I think he's passive? YES.

Is he a coward? Also yes.

Do I think he's an inherently evil man who had horrible intentions and meant to hurt Nikki... no?

Y'all are acting like he should have gotten demon!Nikki to ask real!Nikki for consent first.

Give this man a fucking break.
